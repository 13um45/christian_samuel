Flash utility

Update your pedal's firmware from the browser.

This browser can't flash.

Flashing uses WebUSB, which only Chromium-based desktop browsers support — Chrome, Edge, Opera or Arc on macOS, Windows or Linux. Firefox, Safari and every browser on iOS or Android will not work. Open this page in one of those instead.

  1. Plug the pedal into this computer with USB.
  2. Press RESET, then immediately press BOOT. Do not hold BOOT down during RESET — tap it just after. The dot below turns green once the pedal is ready.
  3. Press Flash utility. The first time, the browser asks you to pick the pedal from a list — instructions appear here when it does. After that, Flash goes straight through.

Why the BOOT tap: after RESET the pedal only listens for about two seconds, which is rarely long enough to also pick it from the browser prompt. Tapping BOOT inside that window holds it open indefinitely, so you can take your time. Without it, press RESET again and pick the device quickly.

Pick your pedal in the browser's list above.
  1. It appears as DFU in FS Mode.
  2. Nothing listed? The pedal isn't in update mode — press RESET, tap BOOT, and it will appear in the list.
  3. Select it and press Connect. Flashing then starts on its own.

You'll only be asked this once — after that, Flash goes straight through.
